// 05 — RECOMMENDATIONS

Priority Action Plan

[P1]
Implement Least Privilege & Separation of Duties
Unrestricted access to PII and payment data is the highest risk. Direct PCI DSS and SOC 2 violation.
[P2]
Enable Encryption for Payment Data
Credit card data is stored and transmitted without encryption — a direct PCI DSS liability.
[P3]
Create Backup & Disaster Recovery Plan
No backups means any ransomware event or system failure could be unrecoverable.
[P4]
Deploy Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
Firewall only controls the perimeter. IDS is needed for internal threat visibility.
[P5]
Strengthen Password Governance & Legacy Maintenance
Update minimum requirements, deploy a centralized password manager, and schedule legacy system checks.
